New NBC Poll Reveals Negative Public Views Toward AI

Category: Public Opinion

Image Source: ChatGPT

A recent NBC News poll highlights a striking disconnect between AI adoption and public sentiment. While a majority of Americans report having used AI tools in recent months, overall attitudes remain distinctly negative: only 26% of respondents expressed positive views, compared to 46% who reported negative perceptions.

Notably, the poll also reveals unexpected demographic patterns. Younger adults (18-34), often assumed to be more technologically enthusiastic, reported the least favorable attitudes toward AI, while older men showed the most positive views toward AI. Taken together, the results underscore a broader “acceptance gap”: AI is rapidly becoming part of daily life, yet public confidence is lagging behind its adoption.
