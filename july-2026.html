The U.S. Launches a Major Quantum Computing Initiative

In May and June 2026, the U.S. government announced a series of measures to strengthen its position in quantum computing, including a $2 billion investment in quantum technology companies and executive orders establishing a national strategy for quantum innovation. The initiative aims to expand domestic manufacturing, advance post-quantum cybersecurity, and accelerate the transition from basic research to practical quantum computers.

Access to Mythos & Fable Models Re-Granted

On June 12, 2026 and related to national security concerns, the U.S. Government issued an export control directive ordering Anthropic to immediately restrict access of foreign nationals to its new models, Mythos 5 & Fable 5. As a result, Anthropic disabled both models for all users worldwide. Following deliberations between the company and government, by June 30 the export controls were lifted, and the models became available again on July 1.

Supreme Court Refuses to Hear AI Copyright Case: Human-Only Rule Stands

In 2025, the U.S. Court of Appeals affirmed the decisions of the U.S. Copyright Office and the U.S. District Court denying copyright protection for a work of art generated entirely by AI. The courts concluded that the work lacked the required element of human authorship. In March 2026, the U.S. Supreme Court denied the plaintiff’s petition for a writ of certiorari, effectively ending the case and leaving intact the current legal position that works created entirely by AI are not eligible for copyright protection in the United States.

EU AI Act Implementation Timeline Delayed

Several months before major compliance obligations were due to take effect, the EU has agreed to postpone the application of certain provisions of the AI Act. The changes delay many of the obligations applicable to high-risk AI systems, while preserving the Act’s core risk-based framework. Stand-alone high-risk systems are now expected to comply beginning December 2, 2027 rather than August 2026, while high-risk AI systems embedded in regulated products will generally follow in August 2028. The agreement also postpones national AI regulatory sandbox requirements and includes measures aimed at reducing compliance burdens for businesses.

Technological Updates

Apple Unveils a Fully Rebuilt Siri Powered by Google’s Gemini

On June 8, Apple announced Siri AI, a significant update to Siri as part of its broader Apple Intelligence initiative. The enhanced assistant offers more natural conversations, on-screen awareness, and deeper integration with apps and system functions. Media reports also indicated that Apple has explored licensing Google’s Gemini models to support certain AI capabilities. Some of Apple’s Intelligence features, however, will roll out later or remain unavailable in certain jurisdictions, including parts of the EU and China, because of regulatory and market-specific requirements.

North Korea Reportedly Tests AI-Guided Missiles for the First Time

North Korea has tested missiles guided by artificial intelligence for the first time, according to state media. The reported upgrades focused on guidance, navigation, and automated launch and control functions. The systems tested included a cruise missile, ballistic missiles, and 240mm rocket artillery that Pyongyang said had been fitted with ultra-precision navigation technology. State media said the changes were meant to make the weapons more accurate and better suited to modern combat, and it pointed to improvements in automated launch systems. The claims have not been independently verified.

Also in the News

ClickUp CEO Outlines the AI-Era Workforce

In June 2026, ClickUp CEO Zeb Evans outlined his vision for how AI will transform the workforce, following a major restructuring that included a 22% reduction in headcount. Evans argues that AI will create new categories of work centered on directing, managing, and collaborating with AI systems. He identified three emerging roles: builders, system managers, and front-liners. He also announced compensation bands reaching $1 million a year for employees who create exceptional value through AI-enabled work.
