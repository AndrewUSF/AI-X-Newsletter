A Non-Scientist Developed a Personalized Cancer Treatment with the Help of AI

Category: Healthcare / Research

Image Source: ChatGPT

Paul Conyngham, a computer engineer with no background in medicine, set out to develop a treatment for his dog’s cancer after conventional options had failed. He used ChatGPT to help brainstorm a research plan, including the idea of comparing his dog’s DNA with that of the tumor to identify potential causes. Acting on this approach, Conyngham collaborated with researchers at an Australian genomics center to carry out the a DNA sequencing of his dog.

He then employed various AI tools, including AlphaFold, to analyze the data, identify a potential causal mutation, and explore how its effects might be addressed. Finally, he used computational methods to design a tailored mRNA-based treatment, which was administered to the dog and reportedly led to a significant reduction in tumor size.

Implications/bottom line: While the results are anecdotal, the case illustrates how the democratization of AI technology may support impactful exploratory research outside traditional settings.
