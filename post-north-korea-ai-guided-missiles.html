North Korea Reportedly Tests AI-Guided Missiles for the First Time

Category: Technology / National Security

Image Source: ChatGPT

North Korea has announced its first test of missiles guided by artificial intelligence, describing it as a step in its military modernization program. According to state media, the launches were overseen by Kim Jong Un, who said the weapons had been upgraded to meet the conditions of modern warfare. The reporting focused on new AI-assisted guidance and navigation systems.

The tests involved several systems. State media reported a nuclear-capable cruise missile, ballistic missiles carrying a “special mission warhead,” and 240mm rocket artillery fitted with advanced navigation technology. North Korea said these weapons had been upgraded with new guidance and navigation systems intended to improve their precision.

According to the Korean Central News Agency, the launches also showed improvements to automated launch systems, not only to the missiles themselves. State media presented this as an effort to make North Korea’s strike capabilities faster and more precise. As with other North Korean weapons announcements, the claims were not independently verified.
