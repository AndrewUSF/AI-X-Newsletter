Meta Granted a Patent for Simulating Post-Mortem Social Media Activity

Category: Social Media / Ethics

Image Source: ChatGPT

Meta was granted a U.S. patent describing an AI-driven system designed to simulate a user’s presence on social media. The system would rely on LLMs trained on extensive user-specific data, such as posts, comments, likes, and messages, to replicate how a person typically communicates online.

The proposed technology could then generate posts, reply to comments, and engage in direct messages. More advanced versions outlined in the patent include the ability to simulate audio or video interactions. According to Meta, the grant of the patent does not necessarily indicate that the company will choose to pursue the patented invention.
